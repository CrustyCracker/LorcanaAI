Understanding the Stats

Presence %
How many decks of this ink combo include this card. 80% means 4 out of every 5 decks run it.
Top-cut Performance (Xx)
How often decks with this card make the top 25% of their event, relative to the ink combo average. 1.00x = average top-cut rate — above 1.00x means the card correlates with top finishes, below means it doesn't.
Avg. copies (×N)
The average number of copies of this card included per deck that runs it.

Card Categories

Staple
Present in over 60% of decks. These are the essential, nearly mandatory picks for this ink combo.
Flex
Solid includes that aren't universal. These cards appear in many builds but vary based on playstyle or meta read.
Fringe
Low presence. Situational or niche picks that see play in specific contexts.
Gems
Low presence (≤10%) but high performance (≥1.10x), with at least 5 decks. Hidden gems that punch above their popularity — worth considering.
Traps
High presence (≥30%) but below-average performance (≤0.90x), with at least 5 decks. Popular cards that may be overplayed or overrated in practice.
